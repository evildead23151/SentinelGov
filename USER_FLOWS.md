# Operational User Flows: Detailed Guide

This document provides a deep dive into the operational workflows for the different personas using the SentinelGov platform.

---

## 👨‍✈️ Persona 1: The Investigator (Security & Audit)
**Goal**: Identify, analyze, and mitigate procurement risks.

### Flow A: The "Red-Flag" Response
1. **Entry**: Investigator logs in. The **Dashboard** immediately highlights a red spike in "Risk Exposure".
2. **Identification**: Navigate to the **Detection Center**. The list is sorted by "Risk Score". The top entry (e.g., *9MM Ammunition*) shows a score of 80+.
3. **Forensic Audit**: Click the **Audit** button. 
   - Review the **Forensic Signal** section: system notes "Rank 3 Awarded", "Spread +15%", "Over Budget".
   - Use the **AI Agent**: Type `ANALYZE_ALERT(x)`. The AI summarizes the tender's economic cluster and confirms the deviation.
4. **Enforcement**: Click **FREEZE**. 
   - **System Effect**: The linked transaction is locked to `ON_HOLD`. Payouts for this invoice are programmatically blocked in the financial system.
   - **Verification**: Return to Dashboard. The **Payments on Hold** KPI has increased by the invoice amount.

### Flow B: Entity Relationship Analysis
1. **Entry**: Open the **Entity Graph**.
2. **Link Analysis**: Locate the winning vendor of a high-risk tender.
3. **Anomaly Check**: Expand the node to see all tenders won by this vendor. If the graph shows a "Star" pattern (one vendor linked to all tenders in a department), it signals potential vendor capture.

## 🏢 Persona 2: The Finance Lead (Treasury & Disbursement)
**Goal**: Authorized release of funds for cleared expenditures.

### Flow C: The Authorization Gate
1. **Entry**: Finance Lead logs in. Dashboard shows $XXX.XM in **Payments on Hold**.
2. **Review**: Navigate to the **Treasury Dashboard**. 
   - This view focuses ONLY on transactions requiring disbursement.
3. **Verification**: Locate a transaction that has been "Cleared" by an Investigator (no active alerts).
4. **Release**: Click **Confirm Disbursement**.
   - A modal appears for a mandatory **Justification Note**.
   - **System Effect**: Transaction status updates to `CLEARED`. All linked alerts are automatically resolved.
   - **Verification**: Funds are logically released to the vendor. The "Payments on Hold" KPI decreases.

---

## 🌐 Persona 3: General Public (Transparency & Trust)
**Goal**: Verify government spending integrity.

### Flow D: The Transparency Audit
1. **Entry**: Access the **Public Transparency Board** (No login required).
2. **Scan**: Scroll through the immutable list of **Sanctioned** procurement records. 
   - Note the **Department**, **Vendor**, and **Sanctioned Amount**.
3. **Integrity Check**: Notice the **Cryptographic Hash** badge on each record. This ensures that the data displayed has not been tampered with since its official sanction.
4. **Verification**: Public can search for specific departments to see where tax money is being allocated in real-time.

---

## 🤖 Flow 5: The "Procedural Agent" Interaction
**Goal**: High-speed data retrieval for auditors.

1. **Prompt**: `SUMMARIZE_TENDER(TND-xxxx)`
2. **Reasoning**: The AI Node fetches the Tender amount, the lowest bid, and the winning bid.
3. **Response**: 
   - "Tender TND-xxxx award is economically justified. Winner is L1. Price within 2% of budget."
   - Includes mandatory disclaimer: *"Auditor-Safe Report generated by AI Node."*
