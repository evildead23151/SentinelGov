# Operational User Flows: Detailed Guide (v3.1.1)

This document provides a deep dive into the operational workflows for the different personas using the SentinelGov platform.

---

## 👨‍✈️ Persona 1: The Investigator (Security & Audit)
**Goal**: Identify, analyze, and mitigate procurement risks through administrative holds.

### Flow A: The "Red-Flag" Response
1. **Entry**: Investigator logs in. The **Dashboard** immediately highlights a red spike in "Risk Exposure".
2. **Identification**: Navigate to the **Detection Center**. The list is sorted by "Risk Score".
3. **Audit**: Click the **Audit** button. 
   - Review signals like "Rank 3 Awarded", "Spread +15%", "Over Budget".
   - Use the **ML-Assisted Support Layer** (AI Agent) for summary.
4. **Enforcement**: Click **HOLD**. 
   - **System Effect**: This sets transaction state to `ON_HOLD`, preventing authorization for disbursement. It introduces an administrative hold prior to release.
   - **Verification**: The **Payments on Hold** KPI increases by the invoice amount.

---

## 🏢 Persona 2: The Finance Lead (Treasury & Disbursement)
**Goal**: Authorized release of funds for cleared expenditures.

### Flow C: The Authorization Gate
1. **Entry**: Finance Lead logs in. Dashboard shows $XXX.XM in **Payments on Hold**.
2. **Review**: Navigate to the **Treasury Dashboard**. 
3. **Verification**: Locate a transaction that has been "Cleared" by an Investigator.
4. **Release**: Click **Confirm Disbursement** with a mandatory **Justification Note**.
   - **System Effect**: This resolves the administrative hold. Transaction status updates to `CLEARED`, providing authorization for disbursement.

---

## 🌐 Persona 3: General Public (Transparency & Trust)
**Goal**: Verify government spending integrity.

### Flow D: The Transparency Audit
1. **Entry**: Access the **Public Transparency Board**.
2. **Scan**: Scroll through the immutable list of **Sanctioned** procurement records. 
3. **Integrity Check**: Notice the **Cryptographic Hash** badge on each record. This ensures that the data displayed has not been tampered with since its official sanction.

---

## 🤖 Flow 5: The "Procedural Agent" Interaction
**Goal**: High-speed data retrieval for auditors (Statistical Support).

1. **Prompt**: `SUMMARIZE_TENDER(TND-xxxx)`
2. **Response**: 
   - "Tender TND-xxxx award is economically justified. Winner is L1."
   - Includes mandatory disclaimer: *"Auditor-Safe Report generated by Statistical Support Layer."*
